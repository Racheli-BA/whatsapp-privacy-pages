WhatsApp Data Deletion Instructions

מבנה מקבוצת גפן ביטוח ופיננסים operates a dedicated WhatsApp Business lead-intake integration.

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your interaction with our dedicated WhatsApp Business intake number.

How to submit a deletion request

Send an email to:

Rba.cpa1204@gmail.com

Use the subject:

WhatsApp Data Deletion Request

Please include:

  • The WhatsApp phone number that contacted our dedicated intake number
  • The approximate date of the interaction
  • A short statement that you are requesting deletion of your WhatsApp-related data

Please do not email identity documents, payment card details, passwords, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

What we will do

After receiving the request, we will identify personal information under our control that is reasonably associated with the request and process the deletion in accordance with applicable law and legitimate security or legal retention requirements.

Information covered

When the relevant intake features are enabled, restricted provisional intake records may contain authorized business text, contact-card names and phone numbers, supported PDF/JPEG/PNG file copies, message identifiers and associated treatment history. Requests may cover this information in isolated testing or the activated business intake workflow. These provisional items do not automatically create ordinary Leads, sales or payment records.

Limited technical metadata may exist temporarily in system or server logs for testing, troubleshooting, security, and integration validation.

Where technically available and legally permitted, relevant personal information under our control, including intake records when present, will be deleted or anonymized using authorized internal procedures. We may retain information where required for legitimate legal, security, or audit obligations.
