Privacy Policy – WhatsApp Lead Intake POC

Effective date: October 14, 2026

מבנה מקבוצת גפן ביטוח ופיננסים (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates a limited Proof of Concept (“POC”) for receiving business lead information through a dedicated WhatsApp Business number.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when a person sends or forwards content to that dedicated WhatsApp number.

1. Information we may receive

When you communicate with the dedicated WhatsApp Business number, WhatsApp / Meta may provide us with information such as:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number or sender identifier
  • Your WhatsApp display name, if available
  • Message content
  • Files or media that you intentionally send or forward
  • Message type
  • Message identifiers
  • Message timestamps
  • Technical delivery and webhook metadata

We do not connect to or automatically read private WhatsApp conversations.

Only content that is intentionally sent or forwarded to the dedicated WhatsApp Business intake number may enter this POC flow.

2. Current POC behavior

The POC operates as an isolated SANDBOX business lead-intake trial. When the relevant trial features are enabled, it may retain intentionally forwarded business text, contact cards containing names and phone numbers, supported PDF documents and JPEG/PNG images, and the metadata needed to group and review that content.

At this stage:

  • The original webhook POC does not yet automatically create production Leads
  • In the isolated SANDBOX trial, message content, selected contact-card fields and sender/message identifiers may be stored as provisional intake records to group forwarded business lead messages, allow an authorized employee to claim an item, and support manager review
  • Provisional intake records are not by themselves production Leads, and identifying a referral source (Leader) requires manager confirmation
  • When media intake is enabled, supported files up to 10MB may be downloaded from Meta and retained in private storage. Files are made available only after an application access check to an authorized manager or the employee assigned to the item. Unsupported or unavailable files may be represented by a status entry
  • The webhook may record limited technical metadata in server logs for testing, debugging, security, and integration validation

Application logs are limited to processing counts and generic error categories. Message contents, contact details, media download URLs, access tokens and other secrets are not intentionally written to application logs.

3. How we use information

Information received through the POC may be processed only for purposes such as:

  • Testing the WhatsApp lead intake integration
  • Confirming that messages reach the correct system
  • Troubleshooting technical problems
  • Maintaining security and system integrity
  • Evaluating the future business lead intake process

We do not sell personal information.

We do not use POC information for unrelated advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Sharing of information

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the POC, including:

  • Meta / WhatsApp
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers
  • Technical service providers used to operate and troubleshoot the system

We use service providers only as necessary for operating the integration.

5. Data retention

We aim to retain POC information only for the minimum period necessary for technical testing, troubleshooting, security, and compliance purposes.

The original webhook POC did not retain message content. The SANDBOX trial may retain authorized business text, selected contact-card details, supported files and related processing history in restricted, non-public storage for the limited trial and applicable obligations. A private file copy can remain available after its temporary Meta download URL expires. We aim to limit retention to what is necessary and review retained trial information when the trial ends. You may request deletion using the contact information below.

Limited technical logs may be retained by the hosting infrastructure according to the applicable system and platform settings.

6. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through the POC.

Access to technical systems and business information is limited to authorized personnel. Automated text redaction is designed to remove recognized payment-card patterns. It does not inspect or redact the contents of documents or images. Do not send payment-card details, passwords or unnecessary sensitive information in text, contact cards or files.

This policy applies to the current WhatsApp Lead Intake POC.

This update describes the optional SANDBOX contact-card and private-file trial. Any expansion to production lead creation or additional processing requires a separate review. The isolated SANDBOX trial does not authorize production deployment.
