Privacy Policy – WhatsApp Business Lead Intake

Effective date: October 14, 2026

מבנה מקבוצת גפן ביטוח ופיננסים (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates a business lead-intake integration using a dedicated WhatsApp Business number. This notice covers isolated testing and the limited production intake workflow when activated.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when a person sends or forwards content to that dedicated WhatsApp number.

1. Information we may receive

When you communicate with the dedicated WhatsApp Business number, WhatsApp / Meta may provide us with information such as:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number or sender identifier
  • Your WhatsApp display name, if available
  • Message content
  • Files or media that you intentionally send or forward
  • Message type
  • Message identifiers
  • Message timestamps
  • Technical delivery and webhook metadata

We do not connect to or automatically read private WhatsApp conversations.

Only content that is intentionally sent or forwarded to the dedicated WhatsApp Business intake number may enter this intake flow.

2. Intake behavior

When the relevant intake features are enabled in an isolated test or approved production workflow, the integration may retain intentionally forwarded business text, contact cards containing names and phone numbers, supported PDF documents and JPEG/PNG images, and the metadata needed to group and review that content.

At this stage:

  • Completed message groups may appear as provisional WhatsApp intake items in the shared lead list. They do not automatically create ordinary production Lead, sale or payment records
  • In the enabled business intake workflow, message content, selected contact-card fields and sender/message identifiers may be stored as provisional intake records to group forwarded business lead messages, allow an authorized employee to claim an item, and support manager review
  • Provisional intake records are not by themselves ordinary Lead records, and identifying a referral source (Leader) requires manager confirmation
  • When media intake is enabled, supported files up to 10MB may be downloaded from Meta and retained in private storage. Files are made available only after an application access check to an authorized manager or the employee assigned to the item. Unsupported or unavailable files may be represented by a status entry
  • The webhook may record limited technical metadata in server logs for testing, debugging, security, and integration validation

Application logs are limited to processing counts and generic error categories. Message contents, contact details, media download URLs, access tokens and other secrets are not intentionally written to application logs.

3. How we use information

Information received through the dedicated business intake number may be processed only for purposes such as:

  • Receiving and grouping authorized business lead messages
  • Allowing permitted staff to claim intake items and record treatment notes
  • Supporting manager review of referral sources
  • Testing the WhatsApp lead intake integration
  • Confirming that messages reach the correct system
  • Troubleshooting technical problems
  • Maintaining security and system integrity
  • Evaluating the future business lead intake process

We do not sell personal information.

We do not use intake information for unrelated advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Sharing of information

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the integration, including:

  • Meta / WhatsApp
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers
  • Technical service providers used to operate and troubleshoot the system

We use service providers only as necessary for operating the integration.

5. Data retention

We aim to retain intake information only for the minimum period necessary for technical testing, troubleshooting, security, and compliance purposes.

The original webhook POC did not retain message content. The enabled business intake workflow may retain authorized business text, selected contact-card details, supported files and related processing history in restricted, non-public storage for the business intake workflow and applicable obligations. A private file copy can remain available after its temporary Meta download URL expires. We aim to limit retention to what is necessary and review retained information when it is no longer needed for that workflow. You may request deletion using the contact information below.

Limited technical logs may be retained by the hosting infrastructure according to the applicable system and platform settings.

6. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through the integration.

Access to technical systems and business information is limited to authorized personnel. Automated text redaction is designed to remove recognized payment-card patterns. It does not inspect or redact the contents of documents or images. Do not send payment-card details, passwords or unnecessary sensitive information in text, contact cards or files.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy applies to content intentionally sent or forwarded to the dedicated WhatsApp Business intake number.

This update describes text, contact-card and private-file intake, provisional work items, permitted employee treatment and manager review. Automatic creation of ordinary Leads, sales or payment records and any additional use require a separate review. Publication of this notice does not by itself activate a feature or authorize a technical deployment.
