Privacy Policy – WhatsApp Lead Intake POC

Effective date: October 13, 2026

מבנה מקבוצת גפן ביטוח ופיננסים (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates a limited Proof of Concept (“POC”) for receiving business lead information through a dedicated WhatsApp Business number.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when a person sends or forwards content to that dedicated WhatsApp number.

1. Information we may receive

When you communicate with the dedicated WhatsApp Business number, WhatsApp / Meta may provide us with information such as:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number or sender identifier
  • Your WhatsApp display name, if available
  • Message content
  • Files or media that you intentionally send or forward
  • Message type
  • Message identifiers
  • Message timestamps
  • Technical delivery and webhook metadata

We do not connect to or automatically read private WhatsApp conversations.

Only content that is intentionally sent or forwarded to the dedicated WhatsApp Business intake number may enter this POC flow.

2. Current POC behavior

The POC currently validates the integration and is preparing a strictly isolated SANDBOX text-intake trial. When enabled, the trial may securely retain text messages and basic processing metadata from authorized business senders, including customer contact information included in those messages.

At this stage:

  • The original webhook POC does not yet automatically create production Leads
  • In the isolated SANDBOX text trial, if and when enabled, message content and sender/message identifiers may be stored as provisional intake records to group forwarded business lead messages and support manual manager review
  • Provisional intake records are not by themselves production Leads, and identifying a referral source (Leader) requires manager confirmation
  • Files and media are not included in the current text-only storage trial
  • The webhook may record limited technical metadata in server logs for testing, debugging, security, and integration validation

Such technical metadata may include whether a message was received, message type, sender identifier, and similar non-secret operational information.

3. How we use information

Information received through the POC may be processed only for purposes such as:

  • Testing the WhatsApp lead intake integration
  • Confirming that messages reach the correct system
  • Troubleshooting technical problems
  • Maintaining security and system integrity
  • Evaluating the future business lead intake process

We do not sell personal information.

We do not use POC information for unrelated advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Sharing of information

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the POC, including:

  • Meta / WhatsApp
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers
  • Technical service providers used to operate and troubleshoot the system

We use service providers only as necessary for operating the integration.

5. Data retention

We aim to retain POC information only for the minimum period necessary for technical testing, troubleshooting, security, and compliance purposes.

The original webhook POC did not retain message content. The proposed SANDBOX text-intake trial, once enabled, may retain authorized business text content in restricted, non-public intake storage for processing and manager review. We aim to limit retention to what is necessary for that limited trial and applicable obligations. Files and media remain out of scope. You may request deletion using the contact information below.

Limited technical logs may be retained by the hosting infrastructure according to the applicable system and platform settings.

6. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through the POC.

Access to technical systems and business information is limited to authorized personnel.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy applies to the current WhatsApp Lead Intake POC.

If the integration is expanded to production lead creation, media storage, or additional processing, this Privacy Policy will be reviewed and updated before those features are enabled. The isolated SANDBOX text-intake trial does not authorize production deployment.
